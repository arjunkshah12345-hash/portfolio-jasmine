If you searched for my name, here's the full story.

I'm **Arjun Shah**, a 14-year-old developer, founder, and builder based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I build artificial intelligence systems, autonomous software agents, context compression engines, and design tools.

I started coding at 7, shipping products by 12, and winning startup competitions by 13. Here's how it happened.

---

## The Beginning

I started coding at age 7. My first language was Python, learned on Tynker — a platform that teaches programming through puzzles. I remember the first time I made something appear on screen. It felt like magic. Not the superficial kind — the kind where you realize you *create something from nothing*.

By 9, I had moved past tutorials. I started building small scripts and automation tools that solved problems I actually had. The shift from consumer to creator happened without me noticing. I just kept building because it was fun.

## Neural Networks at 10

At 10, I discovered neural networks. I didn't fully understand the math — honestly, I still don't understand all of it — but I understood enough. I built small models, trained them on toy datasets, and watched them learn. The idea that code could improve itself was intoxicating.

That early exposure shaped everything I do today. Most of my current projects involve systems that learn, adapt, and improve over time.

## Shipping at 12

By 12, I was shipping. My first real product was a typing club bot — a small automation tool. It wasn't glamorous, but it taught me something critical: **building is easy. Shipping is hard. Getting someone to use what you built is the real challenge.**

That lesson stuck. It's why I ship fast and iterate relentlessly.

## Winning Stanford GSB LISA at 13

At 13, I built **rooted.ai** — an AI-powered platform for exploring natural health knowledge. I entered the Stanford Graduate School of Business LISA startup competition.

I didn't think I would win. But I did.

Standing on that stage as a 13-year-old pitching an AI health platform to Stanford professors was surreal. It taught me that **age is irrelevant. Ideas and execution are what matter.**

## What I'm Building Now

At 14, everything accelerated. Here are my current projects:

### Loopy — Autonomous Software Engineer
An AI agent that researches, plans, builds, fixes, and loops until the product is done. Built around short execution cycles so the agent re-reads the repo state after every step.
→ [loopy.yachts](https://loopy.yachts)

### Supercompress — Neural Context Compression
Compresses long agent history while preserving decisions, constraints, and state that actually matter. Cuts token usage by ~65% while retaining ~98.7% of important information. Ranks #1 on Google for its category.
→ [supercompress.dev](https://supercompress.dev)

### ascii-skill — ASCII Art for AI Agents
A world-class ASCII art engine with zero dependencies. Scenes, UI, 3D rotating objects, particle systems, wireframe terrain — all in pure Python. 124+ pieces generated, 420+ keywords, 15 style presets.
→ [gitlab.com/arjunkshah/ascii-skill](https://gitlab.com/arjunkshah/ascii-skill)

### Pincer — Accessibility Extension
A Chrome extension that modifies page content to support dyslexic and neurodivergent users.
→ [trypincer.netlify.app](https://trypincer.netlify.app/)

### Jasmine — AI Frontend with Taste
An AI frontend engineer that designs with taste — better spacing, typography, motion, and hierarchy.
→ [tryjasmine.dev](https://tryjasmine.dev)
