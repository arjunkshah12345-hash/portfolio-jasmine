## Platform Instructions

### Dev.to
1. Go to [dev.to](https://dev.to) and sign up (or log in with GitHub)
2. Click **Write a Post** (top right)
3. Paste the entire contents of `devto-who-is-arjun-shah.md`
4. Change `published: false` to `published: true` in the front matter
5. Upload the `about.jpg` from your site as the cover image
6. Click **Publish**
7. In the tags section, add: `ai`, `programming`, `webdev`, `startup`, `python`

### Medium
1. Go to [medium.com](https://medium.com) and sign up
2. Click your profile → **Write a Story**
3. Paste the entire contents of `medium-why-i-code.md`
4. Add a cover image (use the about.jpg from your site: `https://arjunshah.xyz/about.jpg`)
5. Add tags: `AI`, `Programming`, `Coding`, `Startup`, `Technology`
6. Click **Publish**
7. **Important:** In settings, add `https://arjunshah.xyz/writing` as the canonical URL

### LinkedIn Article
1. Go to [linkedin.com](https://linkedin.com)
2. Click **Write an article** on the homepage
3. Paste `linkedin-winning-stanford-lisa.md` content
4. Add a headline image
5. Click **Publish**
6. After publishing, also post the short version from `linkedin-86-repos.md` as a regular post

### Hashnode
1. Go to [hashnode.com](https://hashnode.com) and sign up
2. Create a blog (name it something like "Arjun Shah — Building in Public")
3. Click **New Story**
4. Paste `hashnode-building-loopy.md` content
5. The front matter already has tags and cover image set
6. Click **Publish**
